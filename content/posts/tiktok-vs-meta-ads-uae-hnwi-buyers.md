# Where UAE Ultra-HNWIs Actually Spend Attention: Meta, TikTok, or LinkedIn?

There is a persistent myth among conservative executive boards in the UAE: *"Our clients are ultra-wealthy individuals; they do not use TikTok, and they only read LinkedIn."*

Our proprietary media spend data across luxury real estate, private banking, and supercar rentals reveals a radically different reality.

## The UAE Attention Economy Breakdown

- **TikTok in the UAE**: Over 84% of smartphone owners in Dubai interact with TikTok daily, including prominent Emirati family business owners, private collectors, and international founders. Short-form walkthroughs of penthouses often outperform high-production YouTube commercials by 3.4x in raw inquiry volume.
- **Instagram / Meta**: Still the sovereign king of aesthetic brand prestige and high-trust retargeting.
- **LinkedIn**: Indispensable for corporate B2B governance, corporate tax restructuring, and enterprise SaaS, but significantly higher CPMs.

### The Omnichannel Allocation Framework

For every 100,000 AED monthly ad budget in the UAE, our recommended allocation:
- **50% Meta (Instagram Reels & Feed)**: Broad conversion engine & lookalike targeting.
- **30% TikTok**: High-velocity visual hooks & localized Khaleeji trend integration.
- **20% Google Search & LinkedIn**: High-intent capture & C-suite pipeline validation.
